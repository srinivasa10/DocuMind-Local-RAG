Northstar Services Employee Handbook

Welcome to Northstar Services. This handbook applies to all full-time employees in India unless a local policy says otherwise.

Working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Company equipment must be used only for approved business purposes.

All employees must complete security-awareness training within 30 days of joining and once every calendar year thereafter.
