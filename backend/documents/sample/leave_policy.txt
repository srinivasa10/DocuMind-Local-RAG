Northstar Services Leave Policy

Annual leave: Full-time employees receive 20 paid annual-leave days per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly and may be requested through the HR portal at least five working days in advance when practical.

Carry forward: A maximum of five unused annual-leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Any balance above five days expires on 31 March.

Sick leave: Employees receive 10 paid sick-leave days per calendar year. A medical certificate may be requested for absences of three consecutive working days or more.
